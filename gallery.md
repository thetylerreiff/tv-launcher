# Gallery of themes

All 19 backgrounds and the theme picker, captured at 1920×1080 on the Android TV emulator. Every
background moves: stars twinkle, rain falls, sweeps turn and the metal drifts, which stills can't
show. Colours on a real TV are a little more contrasty.

[Back to the README](README.md)
